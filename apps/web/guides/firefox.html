Move from Arc to Firefox

Bring your Arc Spaces into Firefox as nested bookmark folders.

Import bookmarks

  1. Open the converter and choose your Arc sidebar file.
  2. Review the preview, select the Spaces to keep and download Bookmarks HTML.
  3. Open Bookmarks → Manage bookmarks. In the Library, choose Import and Backup → Import Bookmarks from HTML.
  4. Select the downloaded HTML file. Expand an imported Space and check its Pinned and Today folders.

Move open tabs

Download Handoff and open it. In Firefox, open about:debugging, choose This Firefox, then Load Temporary Add-on and pick Handoff for Firefox.zip. Open Handoff from the toolbar, choose your file and review the plan before clicking Move. Firefox removes the add-on when it restarts.
