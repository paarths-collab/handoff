Move from Arc to Edge

Bring your Arc Spaces into Edge as nested bookmark folders.

Import bookmarks

  1. Open the converter and choose your Arc sidebar file.
  2. Review the preview, select the Spaces to keep and download Bookmarks HTML.
  3. Open Favorites, choose More options → Import favorites, then select Favorites or bookmarks HTML file.
  4. Select the downloaded HTML file. Expand an imported Space and check its Pinned and Today folders.

Move open tabs

Download Handoff for Mac and open it. Copy the Handoff Extension folder somewhere permanent, open Edge's Extensions page, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and pick that folder. Then open Handoff from the toolbar, choose your file and review the plan before clicking Move. The Handoff app can also open tabs straight into each Edge profile without the extension.
