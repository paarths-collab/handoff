Move from Arc to Brave

Bring your Arc Spaces into Brave as nested bookmark folders.

Import bookmarks

  1. Open the converter and choose your Arc sidebar file.
  2. Review the preview, select the Spaces to keep and download Bookmarks HTML.
  3. Open Bookmarks → Bookmark Manager, then choose Import bookmarks from the manager’s three-dot menu.
  4. Select the downloaded HTML file. Expand an imported Space and check its Pinned and Today folders.

Move open tabs

The Tab Mover listing for Chrome Web Store is pending. When available, install the extension in your destination browser, open its importer, choose your file and review the plan before clicking Move.
