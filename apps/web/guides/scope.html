What moves with you

Spaces, pinned tabs, Today tabs, Favorites and folders are included. Arc profile names are labels. Check the preview for warnings about split views, recovered items and unsupported entries.

For history, use the destination browser’s built-in import. Cookies, active logins, Easels, Boosts and Notes are outside Tab Mover’s scope. Manage passwords separately.
