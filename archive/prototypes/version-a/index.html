04 / Privacy

Your tabs stay here

Your browser reads the file locally. The converter’s content security policy blocks outgoing connections. No account or analytics.

05 / FAQ

Common questions

Will this change my Arc sidebar?

The converter reads the file you choose and creates new downloads. You can keep using your original sidebar.

Can I move passwords or browsing history?

Use your browser’s own import tools for history and passwords. The password guide covers the separate steps.

How do I move tabs to Safari?

Download bookmarks HTML and follow the Safari guide.
