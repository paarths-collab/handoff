## Finding and verification

**Medium, fixed.** The published privacy policy at `apps/web/guides/privacy.html:29` and its source at `docs/privacy.md:5` described failed tab URLs in local storage. Inspecting `apps/extension/src/executor.ts:141`, the final save at line 249, and the bookmark executor showed that successful scheduled URLs and skipped URLs are also retained locally. The owner of the change independently confirmed the storage behavior. Both corrected descriptions explicitly name the retained destinations, replacement on a later import, the session limit for Undo and removal by uninstalling. I re-read both corrected files.
