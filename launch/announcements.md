## Product Hunt

**Name** Handoff

**Tagline** Take your Arc sidebar to your next browser

**Description** Turn an Arc sidebar into nested bookmarks, a searchable offline archive, a portable pack or a CSV. Preview your Spaces first. Your file stays on your computer.

**Topics** Productivity, Browsers, Privacy

**First comment**

I built Handoff because the organization of a sidebar can be as valuable as the links themselves. Choose your Arc file, expand a Space in the preview, then download the format you want. Safari’s bookmark import was one of the routes I wanted to cover. I’d love to hear which parts of your setup made the move smoothly. If something looks wrong, share a structural diagnostic or an invented example and keep the real file private.

## Show HN

**Show HN: Handoff – a local converter for Arc sidebars**

I built a small converter for StorableSidebar.json. It reconstructs the sidebar tree and exports nested bookmarks, offline HTML, CSV or a versioned JSON pack. You can inspect the preview before exporting. The conversion page blocks outbound connections with a content security policy.

I’m interested in how people want nested folders represented in browsers that only have flat tab groups. If you have an unusual file layout, structural diagnostics and synthetic examples help me investigate without seeing your private tabs.

**Moving your sidebar into nested bookmark folders**

I made Handoff for Arc users who want to keep the structure of their sidebar. You can drop in a local sidebar file, check your Spaces, then download bookmarks for another browser.
